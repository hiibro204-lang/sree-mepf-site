Home / About

An engineering practice, now building the whole services stack.

SREE ENGINEERING is a contracting firm based in Kukatpally, Hyderabad. We began in engineering and fabrication discipline, built a high-voltage electrical contracting practice, and now deliver mechanical, plumbing and fire safety alongside it - as one integrated MEPF package.

Who we are

Engineering at the core, execution on site.

We exist to solve one problem well: services infrastructure that is engineered and built to stay reliable. That now covers the full MEPF scope - HT and LT electrical, HVAC and ventilation, water and drainage, and fire protection - executed by one team against one programme.

Our approach is deliberately simple: understand the requirement, engineer the right solution, execute with discipline, and hand over infrastructure that performs over the long term.

Electrical contractingHVAC PlumbingFire protectionProject coordination Testing & commissioning
How we grew into MEPF. A contractor who has delivered substations and HT distribution already carries the disciplines that matter most in services: sequence, isolation, testing, documentation and the habit of proving a system before handing it over. We have extended that discipline across the other three divisions rather than starting again with a different culture.
What we are honest about. We are an execution contractor. We coordinate with your consultant's design and we do not replace it. Where a scope needs a specialist designer - clean rooms, medical gas, data-centre topology - we say so at proposal stage, not after award.
How we work

Four stages, every project, every division.

The process does not change with the voltage or the discipline - only the equipment does.

Scope & site study

Load, layout, existing infrastructure and constraints established on site before anything is proposed.

Engineering & planning

Equipment, routing, phasing and statutory interfaces resolved - coordinated services drawing issued before mobilisation.

Disciplined execution

Sequenced installation with safety protocols, verified materials and hold points at every stage that will be hidden.

Test & commission

Nothing energised or handed over until it is proved - with records the maintenance team can actually use.
