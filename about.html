SREE ENGINEERING is a contracting firm based in Kukatpally, Hyderabad. We began in engineering and fabrication discipline, built a high-voltage electrical contracting practice, and now deliver mechanical, plumbing and fire safety alongside it — as one integrated MEPF package.

We exist to solve one problem well: services that stay reliable.

That now covers the full MEPF scope — HT and LT electrical, HVAC and ventilation, water and drainage, and fire protection — executed by one team against one programme.

Our approach is deliberately simple: understand the requirement, engineer the right solution, execute with discipline, and hand over infrastructure that performs over the long term.

  • A contractor who has delivered substations already carries the disciplines that matter in services: sequence, isolation, testing and documentation.
  • We have extended that discipline across the other three divisions rather than starting again with a different culture.
  • We are an execution contractor. We coordinate with your consultant's design; we do not replace it.
  • Where a scope needs a specialist designer — clean rooms, medical gas, data-centre topology — we say so at proposal stage, not after award.

How We Work

Four stages. Every project, every division.

The process does not change with the voltage or the discipline — only the equipment does.

Scope & site study

Load, layout, existing infrastructure and constraints established on site before anything is proposed.

Engineering & planning

Equipment, routing, phasing and statutory interfaces resolved — coordinated drawing issued before mobilisation.

Disciplined execution

Sequenced installation with safety protocols, verified materials and hold points at every hidden stage.

Test & commission

Nothing energised or handed over until it is proved — with records the maintenance team can use.
