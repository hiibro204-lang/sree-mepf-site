FAQ

Questions clients ask

Short answers to the questions that come up before a site visit.

Can we take AMC for only one division?
Yes. Many clients start with fire or HVAC, where a failed test has a consequence. The price per division is on the same rate card as the combined cover.
Do you service systems you did not install?
Yes, after a condition survey. If the survey finds a defect that makes the system unsafe, we say so in writing before taking the contract.
Is the AMC price fixed for the term?
Fixed for the contract year, with the escalation clause stated in the offer. Consumables are priced at the schedule rate, not at spot rates.
