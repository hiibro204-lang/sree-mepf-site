04 — Handover and Compliance

What you receive, and what we clear.

Handover pack

  • As-built drawings per division, signed
  • Approved shop drawings and material submittals
  • Test and commissioning reports with instrument calibration
  • Warranties, OEM contacts and spares list
  • O and M manuals and the training record
  • Statutory approvals and final measurement reconciliation

Approvals we plan for

  • CEIG drawing approval and pre-energisation inspection
  • DISCOM connection, metering and charging
  • Fire NOC — provisional at plan stage, occupancy before use
  • Building permission and occupancy through the single-window route
  • STP consent to operate and rainwater harvesting compliance
  • Labour registrations, insurance and site safety documentation
