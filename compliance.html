Safety

Safety that is planned, not announced.

On every site

  • Toolbox talk and permit to work before the first task
  • Work at height: scaffold tags, double lanyard harness, edge protection, rescue plan
  • Lock-out tag-out for every energised intervention
  • Hot-work permit with a fire watch and extinguisher
  • Temporary power with ELCB, earthing and cable protection
  • Housekeeping as a priced scope item, not a virtue

Managed, not hoped for

  • Safety induction for every worker, recorded
  • PPE issue register and daily checks
  • Incident and near-miss reporting with corrective action
  • Workmen compensation and group insurance in force
  • Subcontractor safety audited, not assumed
  • Emergency contact and hospital route posted at site
FAQ

Questions clients ask

Short answers to the questions that come up before a site visit.

Do you file the fire NOC yourself?
We prepare the fire drawings, as-builts and inspection documents and attend the inspection. The provisional NOC is filed with the building permission through the architect, and the occupancy NOC before occupation — both with us in the room.
Who pays the statutory fees?
Fees are statutory and paid by the owner; our scope is the documentation, liaison and inspection attendance. We state the fee heads in the offer so there is no surprise at the counter.
What if an inspection fails?
We close the defects at our cost where the defect is ours, and re-attend the inspection. That is why the hold points exist — an inspection should be a confirmation, not a discovery.
Are your licences current?
Licence copies, validity dates and the supervisor and wireman permits behind them are issued in the pre-qualification pack, current as on the date of issue.
