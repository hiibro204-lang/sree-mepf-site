External and internal

One contract across the boundary

Most projects split external electrification from internal distribution, and the split is exactly where the arguments start - who owns the cut-out, the plinth, the trench, the cable entry and the metering. We take both sides of that line.

  • External: substation, HT cabling, transformer, earthing, street and area lighting
  • Internal: LT distribution, panels, risers, floor boards, points and fixtures
  • One single line diagram from the utility interface to the last circuit
  • One set of protection settings, approved before charging
