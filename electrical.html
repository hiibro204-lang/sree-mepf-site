Execution

One contract across the boundary

External + internal

One contract across the boundary

Most projects split external electrification from internal distribution, and the split is exactly where the arguments start — who owns the cut-out, the plinth, the trench, the cable entry and the metering. We take both sides of that line.

External: substation, HT cabling, transformer, earthing, street and area lighting. Internal: LT distribution, panels, risers, floor boards, points and fixtures — with one single line diagram from the utility interface to the last circuit.

  • Insulation resistance, earth resistance, polarity and continuity recorded before energisation.
  • Relay and breaker settings approved before charging, not after the first trip.
  • Transformer and DG commissioned to OEM sheets with the load test witnessed.
  • Load balancing and panel schedules handed over as as-builts.
