01 — The Fire Path

How a fire system responds.

The pump set is three pressure bands, not one pump. The jockey holds pressure and starts on a small drop; the main pump starts on a further drop and stops only by hand; the standby takes over on failure or low pressure. That logic is what an inspector tests first.

Execution

What the committee actually does

Inspection

What the committee actually does

Opens the farthest landing valve and checks the pump starts. Confirms the flow switch shows at the panel. Runs cause and effect end to end — alarm, dampers, AHU shutdown, lift homing.

Then measures gate width and head clearance for a fire tender, and walks the means of escape, emergency lighting and exit signage.

  • Hydraulic test, then flow test at the farthest outlet, witnessed.
  • Pump performance plotted against the curve, not assumed.
  • Cause-and-effect matrix tested point by point with the consultant.
  • As-builts that match what is installed, handed to the panel at inspection.
