03 — The Interface Problem

Most MEPF failures are interface failures.

A duct that cannot pass a beam. A drain with no slope left. A sprinkler main buried behind a ceiling. None of these are workmanship problems — they are coordination problems.

Coordination

What we do about it

One coordinated services drawing before mobilisation — ducts, trays, pipes, sprinklers and drains resolved at the same level, on paper.

A fixed order of priority: gravity drains first, large ducts second, cable trays third, pressure pipes fourth, sprinklers last.

  • Hold points at every stage that will be hidden — sleeves before concreting, levels before ceiling closure, joints before testing is covered.
  • An interface matrix in every work order: who owns sleeves, cut-outs, testing power, water, scaffolding, debris and access.
  • One coordinated as-built set at handover, not four that disagree.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

Short answers to the questions that come up before a site visit.

What does an MEPF contractor do?
An MEPF contractor designs coordination, installs, tests and commissions a building's mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire safety systems, and hands over the documentation that proves they work. At SREE ENGINEERING that means HT and LT electrical, HVAC and ventilation, water and drainage, and fire protection — under one contract.
Do you take a single division or the whole package?
Both. Many clients start with one discipline — usually electrical, where our background is deepest — and extend to the full MEPF package once the working relationship is proven.
Who handles the statutory approvals?
We do. CEIG drawing approval and pre-energisation inspection, DISCOM connection and metering, fire NOC support at provisional and occupancy stage, and the STP consent are part of our scope, not left to the client's consultant.
How do you price a package?
From a take-off against the drawings, with three vendor quotes on approved makes, labour costed from our own muster data, and overheads and margin stated separately. You receive a scope matrix and an exclusions page with every offer.
What happens after handover?
A defect liability period with a named contact, then an optional AMC — preventive or comprehensive — so the systems you paid to prove stay proven.
Do you work outside Hyderabad?
Yes. Execution is pan-India from our Hyderabad base; each state has its own electrical licensing and fire NOC route, and we plan for that at proposal stage rather than discovering it on site.
