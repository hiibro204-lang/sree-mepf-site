02 - The interface problem

Most MEPF failures are interface failures.

A duct that cannot pass a beam. A drain with no slope left. A sprinkler main buried behind a ceiling. A lift that cannot be licensed because nobody pulled the alarm interface. None of these are workmanship problems - they are coordination problems.

What we do about it

  • One coordinated services drawing before mobilisation - ducts, trays, pipes, sprinklers and drains resolved at the same level, on paper.
  • A fixed order of priority: gravity drains first, large ducts second, cable trays third, pressure pipes fourth, sprinklers last.
  • Hold points at every stage that will be hidden - sleeves before concreting, levels before ceiling closure, joints before testing is covered.
  • Interface matrix written into the work order for every subcontractor: who owns sleeves, cut-outs, testing power, water, scaffolding, debris and access.
