Commercial & IT parks

Central HVAC or VRV, high electrical density, UPS and DG, heavy ELV, tenant fit-out coordination, energy metering.

Pharma & life sciences

Clean-room AHUs with HEPA, pressure gradients, solvent exhaust, utility piping, qualification-friendly documentation.

Data centres

Dual-feed intake, 2N distribution, UPS and DG integration, precision cooling, leak detection, clean-agent suppression.

Redundant HVAC with pressure cascades, isolated power, critical UPS, medical gas interfaces, nurse-call containment.
