Numbers we work with

Rules of thumb - checked, then calculated

  • Cooling load: homes 100-150 sqft per TR; offices 300-400 sqft per TR. A chiller gets a load calculation, not a thumb rule.
  • Air quantity: about 400 CFM per TR of cooling; fresh air 15-20 CFM per person.
  • Chilled water: roughly 2.4 USGPM per TR at 10°F delta T.
  • Duct velocity: mains 4-6 m/s, branches 2.5-4 m/s, diffuser neck 2-3 m/s.
  • Duct gauge: by longest side - 24 SWG to 900 mm, 22 SWG to 1500 mm, 20 SWG above.
  • Air changes: car park 6 normal and 12 in smoke mode; kitchen by hood length, not by air changes.
Sequence & testing

Order of work that avoids rework

  • Rigging path and plant-room headroom confirmed before the chiller ships
  • Bases, chiller, pumps and headers - then CHW hydro test at 1.5x before insulation
  • Ducts and risers, then dampers, then diffusers after the ceiling grid is set out
  • Duct leakage test to the specified class
  • Air and water balancing, chiller start-up and part-load run
  • Handover with TAB report, commissioning sheets and O and M
Standards: NBC 2016 Part 8 Section 3, ASHRAE 62.1 for ventilation, SMACNA or IS 655 for duct construction, and the project's own specification where it is stricter.
