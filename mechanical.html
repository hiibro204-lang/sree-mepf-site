Sequence

Order of work that avoids rework

Rigging path and plant-room headroom are confirmed before the chiller ships, not when it reaches the gate.

Bases, chiller, pumps and headers first; the chilled water loop is hydro-tested at 1.5x before insulation goes on. Ducts and risers follow, then dampers, then diffusers once the ceiling grid is set out.

  • Duct leakage tested to the specified class, with the report attached.
  • Air and water balancing by an independent agency — never inside the duct rate.
  • Condensate drains with slope, trapped and tested before the ceiling closes.
  • Chiller start-up and part-load run witnessed, with the OEM sheet signed.
