The division with the smallest share of contract value and the largest share of post-handover complaints. Leaks, pressure and smell are what a client remembers — so we treat this as a testing discipline.

01 — The Plumbing Path

Supply in, sewage out.

Pressure is a design decision. Gravity gives roughly 0.1 bar per metre; past five floors below the tank, zone it with PRVs — and where the terrace cannot carry a taller tank, a hydro-pneumatic set is usually cheaper than strengthening the structure.

Execution

Testing

Proved before the ceiling closes

Stacks are tested at the correct slope before closing — gravity flow and smoke test. Water mains are pressure-tested at 1.5x working pressure, flushed and chlorinated before handover.

Pump head and flow are verified against design, not against the nameplate, and trap seals are checked — the difference between a clean bathroom and a smelly one.

  • Water quality test after chlorination, before the client uses a tap.
  • STP commissioned with an operator briefing and the consent to operate.
  • Tank cleaning, level controls and low-level cut-outs demonstrated.
  • Reuse line labelled and separated from potable, with no cross-connection.
