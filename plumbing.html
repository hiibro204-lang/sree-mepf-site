Division P

Plumbing: two paths, water in and sewage out.

The division with the smallest share of contract value and the largest share of post-handover complaints. Leaks, pressure and smell are what a client remembers - so we treat this as a testing discipline, not a piping trade.

Pressure is a design decision. Gravity gives roughly 0.1 bar per metre. Past five floors below the tank, zone it with PRVs - and where the terrace cannot carry a taller tank, a hydro-pneumatic set is usually cheaper than strengthening the structure.

Design numbers

What a building actually consumes

  • Offices: about 45 litres per head per day
  • Hotels: 180 per bed up to 3-star, 320 for 4-star and above
  • Hospitals: 340 per bed up to 100 beds, 450 above
  • Restaurants: about 70 litres per seat
  • Storage: sump 1-1.5 days of demand; overhead tank about one-third of daily demand
  • STP sizing: about 80% of daily demand, since the balance goes to gardening and washing
Testing

Proved before the ceiling closes

  • Stacks tested at correct slope before closing - gravity flow and smoke test
  • Water mains pressure-tested at 1.5x working pressure
  • Flushing and chlorination before handover, with a water quality test
  • Pump head and flow verified against design, not against the nameplate
  • Trap seal and venting checked - the difference between a clean bathroom and a smelly one
  • STP commissioned with an operator briefing and the consent to operate
