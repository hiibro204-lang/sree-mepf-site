FAQ

Questions clients ask

Short answers to the questions that come up before a site visit.

What does an MEPF contractor do?
An MEPF contractor designs the coordination, installs, tests and commissions a building's mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire systems, and hands over the documentation that proves they work. At SREE ENGINEERING that covers fire fighting and life safety, HVAC and basement ventilation, electrical and substations, plumbing and public health, and ELV and BMS.
Can we take one discipline instead of the whole package?
Yes. Most clients start with one discipline — usually electrical or fire, where a failed inspection has a consequence — and extend to the full package once the working relationship is proven.
Who files the fire NOC and the electrical approvals?
We do. CEIG drawing approval and pre-energisation inspection, DISCOM connection and metering, fire NOC at provisional and occupancy stage, STP consent and rainwater compliance all sit in our scope.
Do you work with our consultant's design?
Yes. We coordinate with the consultant's drawings and issue shop and coordinated services drawings for approval. Where the design is missing or incomplete, we say so before award rather than discovering it on site.
How is a package priced?
Take-off from the drawings, three vendor quotes on approved makes, labour costed from our own muster data, and overheads and margin shown separately. Every offer carries a scope matrix and an exclusions page.
Do you work outside Hyderabad?
Execution is pan-India from our Hyderabad base. Each state has its own licensing and fire NOC route, and we plan for that at proposal stage.
