Ten disciplines. Four divisions. One practice.

Every service below is delivered by the same engineering team, to the same standard, under one contract — so the interfaces between electrical, mechanical, plumbing and fire are our problem, not yours.

Divisions

What sits inside each division.

E

Electrical

External infrastructure and internal distribution in one contract, from the HT intake to the last socket.

  • AIS and GIS substations, 11 KV to 400 KV
  • Transformer packages — receipt to energisation
  • Overhead lines: towers, stringing, jumpering
  • HT intake, RMU, metering and consumer substations
  • PCC, MCC, APFC panels and distribution boards
  • Internal electrification, lighting and power
  • Underground HT/LT cabling, jointing, termination
  • Earthing grids, lightning protection, bonding
Full scope →
M

Mechanical

Comfort cooling and ventilation executed with the discipline of a commissioning team.

  • Chiller plants — air-cooled and water-cooled
  • AHUs, FCUs, DX and ductable systems
  • VRV / VRF systems and refrigerant piping
  • GI ducting, insulation, dampers, diffusers
  • Ventilation, exhaust and kitchen extract
  • Basement car park and staircase pressurisation
  • Smoke extraction and fire damper interfaces
  • BMS point schedule and TAB
Full scope →
P

Plumbing

The division with the smallest share of value and the largest share of complaints after handover — so we treat it as a testing discipline.

  • Water supply, storage and pressure zoning
  • Transfer, booster and hydro-pneumatic sets
  • Drainage, venting and inspection chambers
  • Sanitary fixtures and CP fittings
  • Sewage treatment plants (MBBR / SBR)
  • Water treatment, softening and filtration
  • Rainwater harvesting and recharge
  • Solar hot water and LPG/PNG piping
Full scope →
F

Fire Safety

The only division where a failed functional test stops a building from opening.

  • Sprinkler systems — wet, dry, pre-action
  • Hydrants, hose reels and landing valves
  • Fire pump sets — jockey, main, standby
  • Fire water tanks and reserve
  • Addressable detection and alarm
  • Clean-agent and kitchen suppression
  • Smoke management and dampers
  • Fire NOC drawing and inspection support
