01 - The case

Why one contract beats four.

Four vendors means four programmes, four sets of exclusions, four claims files and a consultant coordinating trades that should have been coordinated by the contractor.

With four vendorsWith one MEPF contract
Each trade blames the ceiling level set by anotherLevels resolved on our drawing before installation
Sleeves and cut-outs become a variation on every tradeOwned in one scope, priced once
Testing power, water and scaffolding billed four timesPriced once, planned once
Interface defects discovered at commissioningInterface tested at each hold point
Four sets of as-builts that do not match each otherOne coordinated as-built set
